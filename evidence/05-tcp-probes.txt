An NSG DROPS. Nothing answers, so the client waits out its own timeout.
A REFUSED connection is the opposite signal: something replied with a RST, so the
host is reachable and nothing is listening -> an application problem.

  timeout  -> suspect the network
  refused  -> suspect the process
